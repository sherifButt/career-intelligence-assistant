Technical Recruiter
Northwind Health — Remote (US/EU)

About Northwind Health
Northwind Health is a growing health-technology company, and our ability to build great software depends entirely on hiring great people. We are looking for a Technical Recruiter who loves the craft of finding, engaging, and hiring exceptional engineers and technical staff.

About the role
As a Technical Recruiter, you will own the full recruiting lifecycle for our engineering and technical roles. You will partner with hiring managers to understand what they truly need, build pipelines of strong candidates, run a thoughtful and humane process, and close the people who will help us grow. You will be an ambassador for Northwind Health at every touchpoint, and a fierce advocate for a fair, high-signal hiring experience.

What you'll do
- Own end-to-end recruiting for a range of technical roles across engineering, product, and platform.
- Partner with hiring managers to define role requirements, scorecards, and realistic candidate profiles.
- Source candidates proactively through outreach, referrals, communities, and your own network.
- Run structured screens and shepherd candidates through the process with clear, timely communication.
- Coordinate and calibrate hiring loops, keeping feedback consistent and free of bias.
- Manage offers and closing, acting as a trusted advisor to candidates throughout.
- Track pipeline metrics and continuously improve conversion, time-to-hire, and candidate experience.

Must-haves
- 4+ years in technical recruiting, with technical recruiting for engineering roles as your PRIMARY skill.
- A track record of hiring software engineers and other technical staff in a competitive market.
- Genuine technical literacy — enough understanding of engineering roles and stacks to have credible conversations and screen effectively.
- Strong sourcing skills across multiple channels, including direct outreach.
- Excellent written and verbal communication and a warm, professional candidate-facing manner.
- Experience partnering with and influencing hiring managers.
- Comfort with an applicant tracking system and data-driven pipeline management.

Nice-to-haves
- Experience recruiting in health-tech, or another regulated or data-sensitive industry.
- Experience building or scaling recruiting processes at a growing company.
- Familiarity with employer branding and candidate-experience initiatives.
- Experience recruiting for remote and distributed teams.
- A network in relevant technical communities.

How to apply
Send your resume/CV and a short note about a hire you are especially proud of to careers@northwindhealth.example. We review every application.
